Industry context

Google Ads strategies change with the buying journey

The right structure depends on lead urgency, customer value, sales cycle, geographic reach and the feedback available from your CRM.

Local and home service businesses

Separate emergency from planned work, tighten service areas, track qualified calls and booked jobs, and shift budgets with seasonality and capacity.

Healthcare and appointment-based businesses

Organize campaigns by treatment or service line, respect advertising policies, and feed booked and attended appointments back into optimization where possible.

Ecommerce and retail

Prioritize Merchant Center health, product segmentation, margin-aware conversion values and inventory signals across Shopping and Performance Max.

B2B and high-consideration services

Distinguish qualified opportunities from raw form fills, map campaigns to buying stages and import downstream pipeline outcomes when the sales cycle permits.

Google Ads agency FAQs

What does a Google Ads agency do?

A Google Ads agency plans campaign structure, keywords, audiences, bidding, budgets, advertising assets and conversion measurement. PPC Guru also reviews search terms, landing-page alignment and lead quality so optimization decisions are based on qualified customers and revenue—not impressions or clicks alone.

How much does Google Ads management cost in Canada?

The total cost normally includes advertising spend paid directly to Google and a separate agency management fee. Fees may be flat, percentage-based or tiered. PPC Guru provides a custom quote after reviewing the account, campaign complexity, target markets, conversion tracking and monthly budget.

Which Google Ads campaign types does PPC Guru manage?

PPC Guru manages Google Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. The recommended mix depends on whether the business sells services or products, the quality of its conversion data, available creative assets, product-feed health, geographic reach and revenue objectives.

How long does Google Ads take to work?

Search campaigns can produce early lead signals after launch, but dependable optimization normally requires more data. Timing depends on search demand, budget, competition, landing-page quality, conversion tracking and sales cycle. The first 30 days should establish reliable measurement and remove obvious structural waste.

Do clients keep ownership of their Google Ads accounts?

Yes. PPC Guru states that clients retain ownership of their Google Ads account, conversion history and campaign data. Advertising spend is paid separately from the agency management fee, and the engagement is month-to-month rather than dependent on an agency-owned advertising account.

Can PPC Guru manage Google Ads campaigns across Canada?

Yes. PPC Guru provides Google Ads management for businesses advertising nationally or across multiple Canadian provinces and cities. Dedicated local landing pages cover city-specific intent, while the national service focuses on Canada-wide strategy, multi-location budget allocation, campaign governance and consolidated reporting.
