Industry context

Google Ads strategies change with the buying journey

The right structure depends on lead urgency, customer value, sales cycle, geographic reach and the feedback available from your CRM.

Local and home service businesses

Separate emergency from planned work, tighten service areas, track qualified calls and booked jobs, and shift budgets with seasonality and capacity.

Healthcare and appointment-based businesses

Organize campaigns by treatment or service line, respect advertising policies, and feed booked and attended appointments back into optimization where possible.

Ecommerce and retail

Prioritize Merchant Center health, product segmentation, margin-aware conversion values and inventory signals across Shopping and Performance Max.

B2B and high-consideration services

Distinguish qualified opportunities from raw form fills, map campaigns to buying stages and import downstream pipeline outcomes when the sales cycle permits.

Google Ads agency FAQs

What does a Google Ads agency do?

A Google Ads agency plans campaign structure, keywords, audiences, bidding, budgets, ads, landing-page alignment and conversion tracking. It then optimizes the account against qualified leads, customers and revenue rather than clicks alone.

How much does Google Ads management cost in Canada?

Your total cost has two parts: ad spend paid to Google and a separate management fee. Agencies may charge a flat, percentage-based or tiered fee. PPC Guru provides a custom quote after reviewing the account, goals, market and budget.

Which Google Ads campaign types does PPC Guru manage?

PPC Guru manages Google Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. The recommended mix depends on the business model, available conversion data, product feed and sales goals.

How long does it take Google Ads to work?

Search campaigns can generate early lead signals after launch, but reliable optimization usually takes longer because tracking, search terms and bidding need enough data. Timing varies by budget, demand, competition, landing page and sales cycle.

Do we keep ownership of our Google Ads account?

Yes. PPC Guru states that clients keep ownership of the Google Ads account and data. Ad spend is paid separately from the management fee, and engagements are month-to-month.

Can PPC Guru manage campaigns across Canada?

Yes. This page covers nationwide and multi-market Google Ads management. PPC Guru also maintains separate local landing pages for businesses searching for a city-specific agency, helping national and local search intent remain distinct.
